## How I work

I bring a business and marketing perspective to technical work: define the problem, map the workflow, choose appropriate tools, establish review points and make outputs usable. AI assistants help with drafting and implementation; outputs must be reviewed and verified before use.

## Scope and confidentiality

The cases are descriptive, not claims of sole authorship or production readiness. The standalone audit prototype uses synthetic data.
